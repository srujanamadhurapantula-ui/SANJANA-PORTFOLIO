Empowering ESG Strategy through People Analytics

This research examines how people-analytics methods — workforce data, engagement metrics, and organisational behaviour patterns — can be used to strengthen ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) strategy and reporting.

The core question: how can companies move ESG from a compliance exercise to something grounded in measurable, people-level data, and what does that connection between HR analytics and sustainability outcomes actually look like in practice.
